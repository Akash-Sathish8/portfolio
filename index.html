MailSentry

Available on the Mac App Store

A macOS app that lets AI assistants work with your email without giving them access to your actual mail account. It runs entirely on your Mac and does not use any external servers.

  • Apple MailKit extension and a sandboxed launchd helper that give assistants a read-only IMAP copy of your mail.
  • Limits that copy to mail from your primary address, cleaned by a MIME parser and several detectors for hidden text.
  • Any action an assistant takes goes through an MCP server with rate limiting and requires your approval.
